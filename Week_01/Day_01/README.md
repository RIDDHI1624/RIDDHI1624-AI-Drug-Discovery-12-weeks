
# Day 1 — RDKit and Cheminformatics Fundamentals

## Topics
- SMILES and molecular structures
- Molecular descriptors
- Morgan fingerprints
- Tanimoto molecular similarity
- ChEMBL data retrieval and validation

## Dataset
100 small molecules retrieved from ChEMBL.

## Results
- Valid molecules: 100
- Morgan fingerprint size: 2048 bits
- Mean molecular weight: 399.83 g/mol
- Mean LogP: 3.01
- Mean TPSA: 79.52 Å²

## Tools
Python, RDKit, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, ChEMBL.
